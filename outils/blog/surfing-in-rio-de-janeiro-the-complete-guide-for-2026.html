Why Surf in Rio de Janeiro?

Rio isn’t just famous for Christ the Redeemer and Copacabana. It’s also one of Brazil’s most consistent surf destinations.

In the west zone of the city — especially around Macumba Beach — waves break almost all year long. We surf here around 300–350 days per year, depending on conditions.

Warm water, sandy bottoms, long beaches, and different peaks make Rio perfect for:

  • Beginners learning their first pop-up
  • Intermediate surfers improving technique
  • Advanced surfers chasing powerful swells

Best Surf Spots in Rio

🏄 Macumba Beach

Located in the Recreio area, Macumba is considered the best surf zone in Rio. It offers long sandy stretches with multiple peaks, making it ideal for surf lessons and progression.
Our surf lessons happen every day at 6:30 - 8:30 -10:30 and 15:30.

🌊 Prainha and Grumari

A protected environmental reserve with more powerful waves. Great for experienced surfers.
Our surf guiding usually happens with one of our coaches. More suitable for advanced surfers because of the localism in the region.

🏖 Canto do Recreio / Recreio

Long beach breaks that work well with bigger swells. If you want to avoid crowds, we have some secret spots.

Best Time to Surf in Rio

The good news? You can surf here year-round.

  • April to October → More consistent swell (autumn & winter season)
  • November to March → Warmer water, smaller waves, perfect for beginners

Even in winter, water temperature stays comfortable compared to Europe or North America. Around 22 degrees Celsius / 71.6 degrees Fahrenheit

Is Rio Safe for Surf Travelers?

Yes — especially in the Recreio / Macumba area, where the surf culture is strong and the atmosphere is more local and relaxed compared to central Rio.

Like any big city, you just need basic awareness. Staying near the beach and with local guidance makes everything smoother.
